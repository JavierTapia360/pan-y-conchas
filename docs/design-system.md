# Design system

The identity is CUATESFARMZ: dominant white, hard black and action red. Product accents stay local: candy yellow/multicolor, jelly pink, ice blue and electric green.

Typography pairs Anton display with Inter body. The 4/8-based spacing scale runs 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128 and 160px. Main content tops out around 1600px.

Motion tokens: 220ms interaction, 380ms component, 800ms editorial; easing `cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1)`. Reduced-motion disables parallax, sweeps, marquee and complex transitions.

Responsive targets: 375–430 phone, 768 tablet, 1024 small desktop and 1280–1728 desktop. Touch targets are at least 44px; safe-area insets protect fixed mobile controls.

Photography provides depth. Corners and shadows are minimal. Graffiti appears only as a small red accent.
